Give

Your generosity makes the work possible — worship, community meals, CREW 29 outreach, and practical help for neighbors in Titusville. Thank you for being love in action.

Where your gift goes

LifePointe Ministries exists to share the love of Christ through worship, outreach, and practical service. Gifts support the full scope of that work:

  • Sunday worship and ministry at the Titusville Civic Center
  • CREW 29 Outreach — meals, clothing, hygiene items, and fellowship for neighbors in need
  • Community meals, clothing assistance, and holiday events
  • Volunteer support and ministry partnerships

If you'd like to designate a gift to a specific area, let us know when you give.
