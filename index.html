Outreach Ministry

CREW 29 Outreach

CREW 29 is the outreach ministry of LifePointe Ministries — providing meals, clothing, hygiene items, fellowship, and hope to our neighbors in need. Volunteers are always welcome to join us in making a difference.

What we provide

  • Meals
  • Clothing
  • Hygiene items
  • Fellowship and hope

Who it's for

Neighbors in need in and around Titusville — no questions asked, no strings attached.

Pastor Royce “Scooter” Morrison

Pastor Royce “Scooter” Morrison was born in Dothan, Alabama, and raised in Titusville, Florida, giving him deep, lasting roots in the community he serves. His passion is helping people know Jesus while serving the Titusville community with compassion, hope, and practical ministry.
