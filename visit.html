What to expect

Here's the honest rundown of your first visit so there are no surprises.

Arrive as you are

No dress code. Jeans, work clothes, business casual — whatever is comfortable is fine.

Where to go

Come into the Civic Center. Someone will be glad to point you the right way — don't worry about figuring it out on your own.

What happens

A worship service — music, a message from Pastor Royce, and time together as a community. Nothing complicated.

Common questions

Can I visit without commiting to anything?

Absolutely. Come, see what it's like, and decide later. There's no pressure to join or sign up.

What if I'm new to church — or haven't been in years?

That's one of the main reasons this page exists. You're welcome exactly where you are. Nobody will make you feel behind or out of place.

Is there a cost to visit?

No. Visiting is free. Giving is an option, not a requirement to walk in the door.

Can I bring a friend or neighbor?

Yes — please. Bring whoever you'd like to come. The more the merrier, and we'll make room.

What if I have a question that's not here?

Call or email us — we'd rather answer a question before you come than have you wonder.
