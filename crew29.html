CREW 29 Outreach

Providing hope, one neighbor at a time

Who we are

CREW 29 is the outreach ministry of LifePointe Ministries — providing meals, clothing, hygiene items, fellowship, and hope to our neighbors in need.

Volunteers are always welcome to join us in making a difference. No questions asked. No strings attached. You are welcome here.

What we provide

Hot Meals

Ready-to-eat meals served with dignity.

Clothing

Clean, in-season items for all ages.

Hygiene Items

Toiletries and personal care essentials.

Fellowship

A warm welcome and listening ear.

Hope

A reminder that you matter, you are seen.

Community

Connecting neighbors to local resources.
