Outreach Ministry

CREW 29 Outreach

Providing meals, clothing, hygiene items, fellowship, and hope to our neighbors in need — right here in Titusville.

What we provide

CREW 29 is the outreach ministry of LifePointe Ministries. We show up for neighbors in need with practical help and genuine care — no questions asked, no strings attached.

Meals

Hot, ready-to-eat meals for people who need one.

Clothing

Clean, in-season clothing for adults and children.

Hygiene items

Toiletries and personal care items people can use right away.

Fellowship & hope

A warm welcome, a listening ear, and a reminder that you matter.

Volunteers are always welcome

We can't do this without volunteers. Whether you can give an hour, a day, or build it into your routine — there's a place for you.

Serve on an outing

Help prepare and hand out meals, clothing, and hygiene items alongside the team.

Pray & encourage

Be part of the fellowship side — showing up, listening, and reminding neighbors they're not alone.

Give goods

Donate clothing, hygiene items, or non-perishable food. See how to give.

Help behind the scenes

Sorting, organizing, logistics — every kind of help matters.
