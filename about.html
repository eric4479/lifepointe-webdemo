About LifePointe Ministries

Love in Action.

Our mission

LifePointe Ministries exists to share the love of Christ through worship, outreach, and practical service.

We believe church is more than a place you attend — it's a community where people grow in their faith, serve others, and find hope together.

Whether you're looking for a church home, seeking assistance during a difficult season, or hoping to serve others, you'll find a welcoming community at LifePointe.

From Sunday worship to community meals, outreach programs, clothing assistance, holiday events, and partnerships, we are committed to putting our faith into action by loving God, serving others, and living out our mission of Love in Action throughout our community.
