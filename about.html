Our mission

LifePointe Ministries exists to share the love of Christ through worship, outreach, and practical service.

We believe church is more than a place you attend — it's a community where people grow in their faith, serve others, and find hope together.

Whether you're looking for a church home, seeking assistance during a difficult season, or hoping to serve others, you'll find a welcoming community at LifePointe.

From Sunday worship to community meals, outreach programs, clothing assistance, holiday events, and partnerships, we are committed to putting our faith into action by loving God, serving others, and living out our mission of Love in Action throughout our community.

Life in Titusville

Pastor Royce "Scooter" Morrison was born in Dothan, Alabama, and raised in Titusville, Florida, giving him deep, lasting roots in the community he serves.

His passion is helping people know Jesus while serving the Titusville community with compassion, hope, and practical ministry.

We'd love to meet you! Join us for worship this Sunday. Bring your family, invite a friend, or simply come as you are. There's a place for you at LifePointe Ministries.

Where we meet

Titusville Civic Center

Home to LifePointe Ministries, the Titusville Civic Center serves as a gathering place for worship, community events, outreach programs, and private rentals. From Monday Night Bingo to holiday meals and community events, the Civic Center is where neighbors come together to connect and serve.
