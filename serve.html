Volunteer

Get involved

Volunteers make this ministry possible. Whether you want to join a CREW 29 outing, help on a Sunday, or serve behind the scenes — there's a place for you.

Ways to serve

CREW 29 Outreach

Deliver meals, clothing, and hygiene items to neighbors in need. This is the hands-on, go-outside ministry of LifePointe.

Learn about CREW 29

Sunday hospitality

Welcome people at the door, help things run smoothly, and make Sunday feel like home for first-time guests.

Community meals

Help prepare, serve, or clean up for community meals and holiday events.

Behind the scenes

Sorting donations, organizing supplies, logistics, setup, and teardown — every kind of help matters.

Prayer & encouragement

Be part of the ministry of presence — showing up, listening, and praying with people.

Skills-based help

Got a skill — fixing things, graphics, writing, driving, cooking? Let us know and we'll find a way to use it.

Before you sign up

Do I need to commit to a regular schedule?

Not necessarily. Some volunteers come regularly and some help when they can. Tell us what works for you — we'll make it fit.

Is there training?

We'll walk you through whatever you need to know for the role you're joining. Nobody is thrown into something unprepared.

Can I bring a friend or family member?

Yes — serving together is a great way to do this. Let us know who's coming and we'll plan for it.

I'm not sure what I'd be good at.

That's normal — tell us and we'll help you find a fit. A willingness to show up and help is the main thing.
